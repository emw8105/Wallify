With regard to the data processing described in more detail below, users and data subjects have the right:

Likewise, under Art. 21 GDPR, users and data subjects have the right to object to the controller's future processing of their data pursuant to Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f) GDPR. In particular, an objection to data processing for the purpose of direct advertising is permissible.

Your data processed when using our website will be deleted or blocked as soon as the purpose for its storage ceases to apply, provided the deletion of the same is not in breach of any statutory storage obligations or unless otherwise stipulated below.

Server / Lambda function data

Wallify is served via AWS serverless infrastructure. For technical reasons, the following data sent by your internet browser may be collected to ensure secure and stable operation: the type and version of your browser, operating system, referrer URL, pages visited, date and time of your visit, and the IP address from which you visited the site.

This data is temporarily held in AWS CloudWatch log streams and is not associated with any other personal data we store. The basis for this storage is Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the improvement, stability, functionality, and security of our website.

Cookies

a) Session / authentication cookies

We use a single first-party cookie named wallify_token_key on our website. This cookie stores a random token identifier that links your browser session to your Spotify OAuth tokens held securely on our servers. The cookie is set with the SameSite=Lax flag and, where served over HTTPS, the Secure flag.

This cookie expires automatically after 30 days. It does not store your Spotify credentials, personal profile information, or listening history directly.

The legal basis for this processing is Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b) GDPR insofar as the cookie is necessary to provide the service you have requested, and Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f) GDPR for the legitimate interest of maintaining a secure and functional user session.

b) Disabling cookies

You can refuse or delete cookies by changing the settings in your browser. If you prevent the installation of the wallify_token_key cookie, you will not be able to use the authenticated features of Wallify, as the cookie is required to maintain your session.

Spotify login and user data

Wallify connects to your Spotify account via the Spotify OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. When you log in, Spotify redirects you back to our service with a short-lived authorization code, which we exchange for an access token and a refresh token. We then fetch the following information from the Spotify Web API to create your account record:

  • Your Spotify user ID
  • Your Spotify display name
  • Your Spotify email address
  • Your Spotify account country

This information is stored in AWS DynamoDB. It is used solely to identify your account within Wallify and is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

During subsequent sessions, Wallify uses the Spotify Web API on your behalf to fetch your top artists and top tracks. This data is not persisted on our servers; it is fetched live and displayed to you only.

You may revoke Wallify's access to your Spotify account at any time by visiting Spotify's connected apps page and removing Wallify. You can also contact us directly to request deletion of the profile data we hold (see Section I for contact details). Upon verified request, your record will be deleted from our database.

Your consent to this data processing is obtained as part of the Spotify OAuth authorization flow, with reference made to this privacy policy. The legal basis is Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. a) and lit. b) GDPR.

OAuth token storage

Your Spotify OAuth access token and refresh token are stored in AWS DynamoDB, keyed by a randomly generated identifier (the value stored in your wallify_token_key cookie). These tokens are only used to make Spotify API requests on your behalf while you are actively using Wallify. Token records are not shared with any third party.

The legal basis for this storage is Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b) GDPR — processing is necessary to perform the service you have requested.
